HOW A STATISTICAL AGENCY IS DEFUNDED OR DISCONTINUED (November 2026)
│   Eight ways a federal statistic stops being produced, ranked by how much of
│   Congress has to agree. Only one of the eight is a repeal.
│   Collection authority: 13 U.S.C. § 141 · 29 U.S.C. §§ 1-2 · 20 U.S.C. § 9545
│   Clearance: 44 U.S.C. § 3507(g), (h) · 5 C.F.R. § 1320.12
│   The system's own rules: 5 C.F.R. pt. 1321 · OMB Statistical Policy Directives

│   ◎ the hinge: the route that needs no vote and leaves no record
│   ✕ a requirement that does not bind  ⚑ a duty with no due date

■ PART A — THE FOUR THINGS A FEDERAL STATISTIC RESTS ON
│
│   A published federal number needs all four of these at once, every year.
│   Remove any one and the number stops. Only the first involves Congress.
│
│  ├─◆ 1. AN AUTHORITY TO COLLECT — a statute that lets the agency ask, and
│  │    sometimes commands it. 13 U.S.C. § 141 for the census; 29 U.S.C. §§ 1-2
│  │    for labour statistics; 20 U.S.C. § 9545 for education statistics.
│  ├─◆ 2. A CLEARED INSTRUMENT — an OMB control number under the Paperwork
│  │    Reduction Act. ⏳ THREE YEARS MAXIMUM, no exceptions.   [44 § 3507(g)]
│  ├─◆ 3. MONEY IN THE CURRENT FISCAL YEAR — an appropriation, or a continuing
│  │    resolution standing in for one.
│  └─◆ 4. PEOPLE AND CONTRACTS — staff to design and analyse, and in most cases
│       a contractor to do the fieldwork. Carried by contract, not by statute.
│
│   ◈ THE ASYMMETRY THIS PRODUCES: the authority is the hardest of the four to
│     remove and the least often attacked. The other three are annual, internal,
│     or both. A statistic is only as durable as its weakest leg, and three of
│     its four legs are renewed by people nobody elected.
│

■ PART B — THE EIGHT ROUTES, RANKED BY HOW MUCH OF CONGRESS MUST AGREE
│
├─◆ ROUTE 1 — REPEAL THE COLLECTION AUTHORITY
│   │   House + Senate + signature. The only route that changes the law on the
│   │   books, and the only one with a published record a reader can find.
│   └─ ◈ No principal statistical agency lost its collection authority by
│        repeal in 2025-26. The route exists and was not used, which is the
│        point: nothing on the rest of this map requires it.
│
├─◆ ROUTE 2 — THE RIDER ON A SPENDING BILL
│   │   One appropriations act. No authorizing committee votes, no hearing on
│   │   the merits of the statistic, and the vote is on the whole bill.
│   ├─ ☐ IT DOES LEAVE A RECORD — the text is in an enacted act.
│   ├─ ★ WORKED EXAMPLE. The Tiahrt rider bars the ATF from releasing the
│   │    trace data police generate when they ask where a crime gun came from,
│   │    except in support of a criminal investigation or a licensing case.
│   ├─ ◎ FOUR WORDS MADE IT PERMANENT. The 2008 omnibus version reads "in
│   │    fiscal year 2008 and thereafter," which the Government Accountability
│   │    Office treats as permanent law. A one-year limitation became a
│   │    standing one without anyone voting on that question.
│   └─ ⚑ THE CARVE-OUT IS THE TELL. The agency may still publish aggregate
│        annual statistical reports. The aggregate survives; the detail that
│        would support an outside analysis does not.
│
├─◆ ROUTE 3 — NEVER APPROPRIATE THE MONEY FOR A MANDATED STATISTIC
│   │   Nobody has to agree. Nobody has to do anything at all.
│   ├─ ★ THE WORKED EXAMPLE IS FORTY-ONE YEARS OLD. 13 U.S.C. § 141(d) says
│   │    the Secretary "shall conduct a mid-decade census of population" in
│   │    1985 and every ten years after. The subsection contains no funding
│   │    condition. It has never been conducted - not in 1985, 1995, 2005,
│   │    2015 or 2025.
│   ├─ ⊘ WHY NOT: § 141(e)(2) provides that mid-decade data "shall not be used
│   │    for apportionment of Representatives" or for drawing districts. The
│   │    one census product with no seats attached is the one never funded.
│   └─ ◎ A STATUTORY COMMAND WITH NO APPROPRIATION AND NO ENFORCEMENT CLAUSE
│        IS A SENTENCE, NOT A SHIELD. Four decades of non-compliance have
│        produced no remedy, no litigation and no repeal.
│
├─◆ ROUTE 4 — LET THE APPROPRIATION LAPSE
│   │   Nobody has to agree; a failure to act is enough. Unlike every other
│   │   route, this one destroys data that already existed to be collected.
│   ├─ ★ WORKED EXAMPLE (2025). The lapse ran 1 October to 12 November 2025.
│   │    Consumer Expenditure Survey collection did not resume until 5 December.
│   ├─ ⊘ THE DIARY SURVEY CANNOT BE BACKFILLED. Its collection months are its
│   │    reference months, so no diary data exist for October or November 2025.
│   │    The agency reweighted September and December by a factor of two.
│   ├─ ◈ THE INTERVIEW SURVEY'S THREE-MONTH RECALL SAVED PART OF IT: roughly a
│   │    third of expenditures lost for July and October, two-thirds for August
│   │    and September, patched by multiplying the surviving months.
│   └─ ⏳ THE NEXT DEADLINE IS LIVE. The continuing resolution signed in early
│        September 2026 funds the government to 11 DECEMBER 2026.
│
├─◆ ROUTE 5 — LET THE CLEARANCE EXPIRE
│   │   Nobody has to agree. This is the one clock on the map that runs against
│   │   the agency by default.
│   ├─ ⏳ OMB "may not approve a collection of information for a period in
│   │    excess of 3 years."                                    [44 § 3507(g)]
│   ├─ ⏳ Renewal is affirmative: the agency must seek public comment and
│   │    resubmit no later than 60 days before the control number expires.
│   │                                           [44 § 3507(h)(1), 5 § 1320.12]
│   └─ ◎ NOBODY FILES AND THE SURVEY STOPS. There is no decision to publish,
│        no notice, no comment period and no appeal, because nothing was
│        decided. ◈ Note the inversion: in the approval process, inaction is
│        always a grant. Here inaction is always a denial. The default runs in
│        favour of the agency both times - toward starting, and toward stopping.
│
├─◆ ROUTE 6 — CUT THE STAFF AND CANCEL THE CONTRACTS
│   │   The agency, or the department above it. No vote, no notice, no rule.
│   ├─ ★ WORKED EXAMPLE (2025-26). The National Center for Education
│   │    Statistics had 102 employees in January 2025. A reduction in force in
│   │    March left all but three terminated; staffing stood at four that
│   │    September, and about a dozen had been hired back by mid-2026.
│   ├─ ⚑ THE SURVEYS WERE CARRIED BY CONTRACTS, SO CANCELLING CONTRACTS
│   │    CANCELLED SURVEYS. ◈ Fourteen named collections and tools were still
│   │    unavailable in August 2026 - among them the household education
│   │    survey, the adult-skills assessment, two high-school longitudinal
│   │    studies, the transcript collection and the education projections.
│   └─ ◈ WHAT CAME BACK CAME BACK AS CONTRACTS. New awards for the
│        postsecondary and baccalaureate studies restored specific collections
│        one at a time. The capacity route is reversible in principle and slow
│        in fact: a longitudinal study restarted is not the study that stopped.
│
├─◆ ROUTE 7 — CHANGE THE METHOD SO THE PRODUCT CANNOT LAWFULLY BE PRODUCED
│   │   The department. An internal order; no rulemaking, no comment period.
│   ├─ ★ WORKED EXAMPLE (2026). Commerce Departmental Administrative Order
│   │    216-26, issued 4 June 2026, prohibits noise infusion and related
│   │    confidentiality methods at the Census Bureau and the Bureau of
│   │    Economic Analysis, prefers coarsening, and permits suppression only
│   │    as a last resort where coarsening is barred by law.
│   ├─ ◎ IT CANCELLED NOTHING AND STOPPED EVERYTHING FOR SEVEN WEEKS. The 2025
│   │    one-year American Community Survey estimates, normally out in
│   │    mid-September, had no release date on 6 August 2026 while the Bureau
│   │    searched for a compliant method. They were rescheduled on 22 October
│   │    and released on 29 OCTOBER 2026.
│   └─ ◈ THE PRODUCT RETURNED AND THE PRECEDENT STAYED. A departmental order
│        about technique is the only route here that can halt a flagship series
│        without naming it, without ending it, and without becoming reviewable.
│
└─◆ ROUTE 8 — SIMPLY STOP PUBLISHING IT
    │   The agency alone. ◎ THE QUIETEST ROUTE, AND THE MOST USED.
    ├─ ★ 133 YEARS, ENDED BY A SENTENCE. The Census Bureau "terminated the
    │    collection of data for the Statistical Compendia program effective
    │    October 1, 2011" - the Statistical Abstract of the United States,
    │    published since 1878. The notice gives no reason.
    ├─ ★ ABOUT 350 PRODUCER PRICE INDEXES, 2025-26. The labour statistics
    │    agency announced it would "end calculation and publication of
    │    approximately 350 indexes," phased across the July 2025, October 2025
    │    and January 2026 reference months. ✕ The notice states no reason.
    ├─ ★ THE AGENCY THAT KEEPS ITS OWN LIST. The Bureau of Economic Analysis
    │    maintains a public page of discontinued and delayed statistics under
    │    one heading for the whole list: "Statistical products and data tables
    │    impacted by budget or resource constraints." Entries run from 2024 to
    │    March 2026 and cover the digital economy, small business, arts and
    │    culture, the marine economy, travel and tourism, the space economy,
    │    dozens of national income tables and a long run of direct-investment
    │    detail. ◈ It is the most honest document on this map and it is a
    │    web page.
    └─ ⊘ NONE OF THESE WENT THROUGH THE FEDERAL REGISTER. No notice, no
         comment, no reviewable final action, no vote.

■ PART C — WHO HAS TO BE TOLD
│
│   The federal statistical agencies have, since 2024, a codified set of
│   fundamental responsibilities at 5 C.F.R. part 1321: produce relevant and
│   timely information, be accurate, be objective, and protect respondents.
│
│  ├─◆ ☐ THE ONE NOTICE DUTY ABOUT SCHEDULES RUNS UPWARD. The rule requires an
│  │    agency to notify the Chief Statistician of the United States of any
│  │    change to the release schedule of a regular and recurring product.
│  ├─◆ ✕ A COMMENTER ASKED FOR SCHEDULE CHANGES TO BE ANNOUNCED PUBLICLY WITH
│  │    AN EXPLANATION. OMB DECLINED TO ADD IT, saying the existing directives
│  │    sufficiently addressed the concern.
│  └─◆ ⊘ AND NOTHING IN PART 1321 REQUIRES ADVANCE PUBLIC NOTICE BEFORE A
│       PRODUCT IS CHANGED OR DISCONTINUED AT ALL.
│
│   ◎ SO THE DUTY TO TELL ANYONE RUNS INWARD, TO AN OFFICIAL INSIDE OMB WHO IS
│     NOT SENATE-CONFIRMED - NOT OUTWARD, TO THE PEOPLE WHO USE THE NUMBER.
│
│   ⚑ AND RECOGNITION ITSELF IS A DESIGNATION, NOT A STATUS. OMB recognizes
│     sixteen statistical agencies and units under 44 U.S.C. § 3562, thirteen
│     of them "principal." The list is sixteen because the Director says it is.
│

■ PART D — WHAT A STATUTORY DEADLINE IS WORTH
│
│   The strongest-looking protection on the map, tested once, in public.
│
│  ├─◆ THE DUTY. 20 U.S.C. § 9545 has required an annual Report on the
│  │    Condition of Education, delivered to Congress and the President by
│  │    1 JUNE, since 2002.
│  ├─◆ ★ 1 JUNE 2025: NOTHING ARRIVED. Reported as the first time the duty had
│  │    been missed.
│  ├─◆ ★ 2 JUNE 2025: THE PRODUCT WAS REDEFINED RATHER THAN DELIVERED. The
│  │    department said it would no longer be a single report; indicators would
│  │    be updated on a rolling basis, with a short highlights volume.
│  └─◆ ✕ CONSEQUENCE: NONE. No penalty, no forfeiture, no cause of action, and
│       no provision anywhere that says what happens if the report is late.
│
│   ◈ A DEADLINE WITHOUT A CONSEQUENCE IS A REQUEST. Compare Route 5, where a
│     missed date stops a survey automatically: the clock that bites is the one
│     aimed at the agency's own permission, not at its duty to the public.
│

■ PART E — THE LEDGER

  ROUTE                        WHO HAS TO AGREE       WHAT IT LEAVES BEHIND
  ============================================================================
  1 repeal the statute         House, Senate, Pres.   a public law
  2 a rider on a spending bill one appropriations act a line in an enacted act
  3 never appropriating it     nobody                 nothing at all
  4 a lapse in appropriations  nobody                 a gap in the series
  5 clearance left to expire   nobody                 an expired OMB number
  6 staff and contracts cut    the agency             a personnel notice
  7 a method order             the department         a departmental order
  8 ceasing to publish         the agency             a date on a web page

  ROUTE                         WHO CAN CHALLENGE IT, AND WHERE
  ====================================================================================
  1 repeal the statute          nobody - it is the law
  2 a rider on a spending bill  nobody - it is the law, for as long as it rides
  3 never appropriating it      no cause of action; the duty is unenforced, not void
  4 a lapse in appropriations   nobody; and the lost weeks cannot be collected later
  5 clearance left to expire    nobody; stopping a collection injures no respondent
  6 staff and contracts cut     personnel and contract forums, by the people cut
  7 a method order              untested; the order is internal to the department
  8 ceasing to publish          untested; no notice, no record, no final agency action

  ◈ Seven of the eight routes need no vote of any kind. Six leave no document a
    member of the public could find by looking. Three destroy data outright
    rather than stopping its publication: the unfunded mandate, the lapse, and
    the cancelled longitudinal study.

■ THE READING, SET OUT PLAINLY
  ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  Nothing on this map requires a repeal.  The one route that changes
  the law needs both chambers and a signature, and in 2025-26 it was
  not used, because it did not have to be.  The other seven are
  available without it: one rides on a spending bill, four happen
  when nobody does anything, and two are internal decisions of an
  agency or a department.
  ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  The statutory mandate is the weakest protection here, not the
  strongest.  A command to conduct a census has gone unperformed for
  forty-one years.  A command to deliver a report by the first of
  June went unperformed in 2025 and the product was redefined
  instead.  Neither carries a consequence, so neither is enforced.
  ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  The clock that actually stops something is the one pointed at the
  agency's own permission to ask questions.  The duty pointed at its
  obligation to the public has no clock at all.
  ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  A federal statistic does not usually die by decision.  It stops
  being renewed.
