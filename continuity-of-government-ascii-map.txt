CONTINUITY OF GOVERNMENT — STRUCTURE OF THE FEDERAL CONTINUITY ENTERPRISE (as of Aug 21, 2026)
│   Three nested programs, one classified spine, and four branch-specific problems
│   ★ = the load-bearing piece is a convention, a chamber rule, or a classified document — not a statute
│
■ THE FEDERAL CONTINUITY ENTERPRISE
│
├─◆ 1. THE THREE NESTED PROGRAMS  (the vocabulary everything else uses)
│   │
│   ├─ COOP — Continuity of Operations ....... one agency keeps performing its own essential functions
│   │   └─ scope: every federal executive department and agency, individually
│   ├─ COG — Continuity of Government ....... the branch as a whole keeps governing
│   │   └─ scope: survival of the executive branch's capacity to direct the nation
│   └─ ECG — Enduring Constitutional Government  all three branches keep their constitutional roles ★
│       └─ scope: the only tier that names Congress and the courts — and the thinnest of the three
│
├─◆ 2. THE POLICY LAYER  (who writes the rules)
│   │
│   ├─ NSPD-51 / HSPD-20 (May 2007) ......... the post-9/11 charter; annexes remain classified ★
│   ├─ PPD-40, National Continuity Policy ... issued July 15, 2016; the operative directive
│   │   └─ establishes the eight National Essential Functions (NEFs) all planning maps back to
│   ├─ Federal Continuity Directive (FCD) ... FEMA/ONCP, December 2023 — planning requirements
│   ├─ FCD — Program Management Requirements  DHS, August 2024 — program-management requirements
│   └─ Continuity Guidance Circular (CGC) ... the non-federal analogue for state/local/tribal use
│       │
│       └─ WHO COORDINATES IT
│           ├─ National Continuity Coordinator  the Assistant to the President for Homeland
│           │   │                               Security and Counterterrorism, by designation
│           │   └─ Homeland Security Advisor ... Stephen Miller (since Jan 20, 2025)
│           │       [whether the NSPD-51 NCC designation formally attaches to him: unconfirmed]
│           └─ Executive agent .............. DHS, through the FEMA Administrator
│               ├─ FEMA Administrator ....... Cameron Hamilton (confirmed Aug 7, sworn Aug 10, 2026)
│               ├─ Secretary of Homeland Sec.  Markwayne Mullin (sworn Mar 24, 2026)
│               └─ Office of National Continuity Programs (ONCP) — the FEMA directorate that
│                   owns federal continuity guidance, training, and assessment
│                   [FEMA has lost ~4,500 employees since Jan 2025 and rescinded its strategic
│                    plan in May 2025 — GAO-26-108427, Aug 2026]
│
├─◆ 3. CONTINUITY OF THE PRESIDENCY  (the one problem with a clean statute)
│   │
│   ├─ Presidential Succession Act of 1947 .. 3 U.S.C. § 19 — VP, Speaker, President pro tempore,
│   │   │                                      then the Cabinet in department-creation order
│   │   ├─ Vice President .................. JD Vance
│   │   ├─ Speaker of the House ............ Mike Johnson (R-LA)
│   │   └─ President pro tempore ........... Chuck Grassley (R-IA)
│   │       [an acting secretary not Senate-confirmed to the office is passed over — see the
│   │        succession map for the full ordered list and the current skip flags]
│   ├─ Twentieth Amendment, § 3 ............ president-elect dies or fails to qualify before noon
│   ├─ Twenty-Fifth Amendment ............... vacancy, temporary transfer, and inability
│   ├─ Designated survivor ................. a convention, not a law ★
│   │   └─ one eligible successor held away from any event gathering the whole line
│   └─ Nuclear command authority ........... survives with the office, not with the building
│       └─ carried on the airborne platforms in layer 5, not tied to any fixed site
│
├─◆ 4. CONTINUITY OF CONGRESS  (the quorum problem — no statute, no amendment)
│   │
│   ├─ THE CONSTITUTIONAL CONSTRAINT
│   │   ├─ Art. I, § 5 ..................... "a Majority of each shall constitute a Quorum"
│   │   ├─ Art. I, § 2, cl. 4 .............. House vacancies filled ONLY by special election —
│   │   │                                    a governor cannot appoint a Representative
│   │   └─ Seventeenth Amendment ........... Senate vacancies MAY be filled by gubernatorial
│   │                                        appointment where state law allows — so the Senate
│   │                                        can be refilled in days and the House cannot
│   ├─ THE HOUSE'S ANSWER
│   │   └─ Rule XX, cl. 5(c) — "provisional quorum" ★
│   │       ├─ on catastrophic circumstances, quorum = a majority of the PROVISIONAL number
│   │       ├─ codified in the 108th Congress; re-adopted via H.Res. 5, 109th Congress
│   │       └─ [constitutionality never resolved — the Speaker declined to entertain the point
│   │          of order, leaving the House to judge its own rule]
│   ├─ THE SENATE'S ANSWER .................. none of comparable specificity; it relies on
│   │                                        gubernatorial appointment to rebuild membership
│   └─ THE REFORM THAT DIDN'T PASS
│       └─ Continuity of Government Commission (AEI + Brookings, 2002–2011)
│           ├─ proposed a constitutional amendment for temporary House appointments — not adopted
│           └─ proposed removing the Speaker and President pro tempore from the succession line
│
├─◆ 5. CONTINUITY OF THE JUDICIARY  (one section of Title 28, and almost nothing else)
│   │
│   ├─ 28 U.S.C. § 1 ....................... nine justices; ANY SIX constitute a quorum
│   ├─ 28 U.S.C. § 2109 .................... if a direct appeal cannot be heard for want of a
│   │                                        quorum, the Chief Justice may remit it to the court
│   │                                        of appeals; other cases simply remain pending
│   ├─ no mechanism to replace justices .... Art. II appointment is the only route; there is no
│   │                                        emergency-appointment provision anywhere
│   └─ lower courts ........................ Judicial Conference / AO continuity planning;
│                                            special sessions may be held at alternate locations
│
├─◆ 6. THE PHYSICAL AND AIRBORNE LAYER  (where the government goes)
│   │
│   ├─ FIXED SITES
│   │   ├─ Mount Weather Emergency Operations Center ... Bluemont, VA — FEMA-operated
│   │   ├─ Raven Rock Mountain Complex (Site R) ....... near Blue Ridge Summit, PA — DoD
│   │   ├─ Cheyenne Mountain Space Force Station ...... Colorado Springs, CO — NORAD alternate
│   │   ├─ Presidential Emergency Operations Center ... beneath the White House East Wing
│   │   └─ The Greenbrier (Project Greek Island) ...... congressional relocation site, WV —
│   │                                                   exposed by press reporting in 1992
│   │                                                   and decommissioned; NO announced successor
│   └─ AIRBORNE PLATFORMS
│       ├─ E-4B Nightwatch (NAOC) ................... 4 aircraft — the "doomsday plane"
│       ├─ E-6B Mercury (TACAMO / ABNCP) ............ airborne launch control and fleet comms
│       └─ E-4C SAOC replacement ................... Sierra Nevada Corp., $13B award Apr 2024
│           ├─ first test flight Aug 7, 2025; testing continues through 2026
│           ├─ planned fleet 6–8 aircraft, up from the 4 E-4Bs
│           └─ contract completion date July 10, 2036 — an 11-year overlap with the fleet it
│              replaces, which is the practical measure of how old the current one is
│
├─◆ 7. THE ESSENTIAL-FUNCTIONS STACK AND DEVOLUTION  (how authority actually moves)
│   │
│   ├─ THE FUNCTION HIERARCHY
│   │   ├─ NEFs — National Essential Functions ...... eight, set by PPD-40; the national floor
│   │   ├─ PMEFs — Primary Mission Essential Fns .... an agency's functions that support a NEF
│   │   └─ MEFs — Mission Essential Functions ....... everything else an agency must keep doing
│   ├─ ORDERS OF SUCCESSION ...................... every agency must pre-designate at least
│   │                                              three deep for each key position
│   ├─ DELEGATIONS OF AUTHORITY .................. pre-signed, so a successor's power does not
│   │                                              wait on a new signature
│   └─ DEVOLUTION OF OPERATIONS .................. transfer of an agency's functions to
│       │                                          geographically separated personnel
│       └─ triggered when the primary staff and the alternate site are both unavailable —
│          the deepest fallback, and the least exercised
│
└─◆ 8. THE UNREVIEWED LAYER  (the part with no external check at all) ★
    │
    ├─ Presidential Emergency Action Documents (PEADs)
    │   ├─ pre-drafted EOs, proclamations, and messages to Congress, held ready for execution
    │   ├─ originated in the Eisenhower administration as part of nuclear COG planning
    │   ├─ 56 in effect as of 2017, up from 48 two decades earlier
    │   ├─ NOT ONE has ever been publicly released; ~6,000 pages withheld in full as classified
    │   └─ no statutory basis, no congressional notification requirement, no judicial review
    └─ classified annexes to NSPD-51 / HSPD-20
        └─ the ECG annex — the tier that governs Congress and the courts — is among them

WHERE THE STRUCTURE IS THINNEST — the four load-bearing pieces that are not law
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  1. THE DESIGNATED SURVIVOR is a convention. Nothing compels it, and nothing sets who it is.
  2. THE HOUSE'S PROVISIONAL QUORUM is a chamber rule of contested constitutionality, adopted
     by the body that would be judging its own compliance with it.
  3. THE JUDICIARY has a quorum floor and no way to refill below it. Six of nine, or nothing.
  4. THE PEADs AND THE ECG ANNEX are classified documents that would govern the moment the
     other three failed — and the branch they most directly constrain has never seen them.
