FEDERAL STATISTICAL SYSTEM TREE — the 13 principal agencies and the thread holding them together
│   Legend: ★ Senate-confirmed  ⏳ fixed statutory term  ○ appointed inside the agency
│   ◐ presidential appointment, NO Senate consent  § statute  ⚑ structural point
│   No officeholders inside the fence by design — dated seat block sits below the chart
│
■ THERE IS NO NATIONAL STATISTICAL OFFICE
│   16 Recognized Statistical Agencies and Units · 13 of them "principal" · 3 further units
│   spread across 9 cabinet departments and 2 independent agencies. Alongside them sit
│   24 Statistical Officials (one per major agency) and roughly 100 other federal programs
│   that do statistical work without being statistical agencies.
│   ⚑ NO COMMON BUDGET LINE, NO COMMON PERSONNEL SYSTEM, NO SINGLE HEAD. Every other
│     wealthy democracy runs a single national statistical office. The United States
│     runs thirteen, and the thing that makes them one system is a memorandum.
│   ⚑ ONLY 4 OF 13 HEADS ARE SENATE-CONFIRMED (★). Nine are appointed by the department
│     that houses them. One — Transportation — is appointed by statute INTO THE
│     COMPETITIVE SERVICE, which makes the head of a statistical agency a civil servant.
│
├─◆ THE COORDINATION LAYER — Office of Management and Budget
│   │   § Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995, 44 U.S.C. ch. 35 · § Evidence Act of 2018
│   │
│   ├─ U.S. Chief Statistician .................... ○ not Senate-confirmed, no fixed term
│   │     Sits in OMB's Office of Information and Regulatory Affairs. Issues and
│   │     administers the Statistical Policy Directives. Approves every statistical
│   │     information collection. Chairs the ICSP. Represents the U.S. at the UN
│   │     Statistical Commission.
│   │     ⚑ THE OFFICE THAT WRITES THE INDEPENDENCE RULE IS THE OFFICE THAT CAN
│   │       REWRITE IT. The post turned over twice between June 2025 and January 2026.
│   ├─ Interagency Council on Statistical Policy .. 30 members · chaired by Chief Statistician
│   │     Started 1989 to connect the heads of the principal agencies; the Evidence Act
│   │     widened it to include the 24 Statistical Officials, 11 of whom also head an
│   │     agency. Advisory. Charters subcommittees (ACS, FSRDC, NSDS, SAP, S-SORR).
│   └─ Federal Committee on Statistical Methodology · the cross-agency methods body
│
├─◆ DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE ................................. 2 principal agencies
│   ├─ Bureau of the Census ....................... ★ ⏳ 5-year term · § 13 U.S.C. 21
│   │     Decennial census · American Community Survey · economic census · apportionment
│   │     ⚑ THE ONLY ONE OF THE THIRTEEN WHOSE OUTPUT IS CONSTITUTIONALLY REQUIRED.
│   │       Art. I, § 2 commands an enumeration; nothing commands the other twelve.
│   │     ⚑ The 5-year term is staggered to start in the second year of a presidential
│   │       term, deliberately, so it does not track the election cycle.
│   └─ Bureau of Economic Analysis ................ ○ appointed inside Commerce
│         GDP · national income and product accounts · balance of payments · trade
│
├─◆ DEPARTMENT OF LABOR .................................... 1 principal agency
│   └─ Bureau of Labor Statistics ................. ★ ⏳ 4-year term · § 29 U.S.C. 2
│         CPI · the monthly jobs report · PPI · JOLTS · productivity · injuries
│         ⚑ The CPI is written into law in a way no other federal statistic is: it
│           indexes Social Security, the tax brackets, TIPS and millions of contracts.
│           An agency with no enforcement power moves more money than most that have it.
│
├─◆ DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE .............................. 2 principal agencies
│   ├─ National Agricultural Statistics Service ... ○ appointed inside USDA
│   │     Census of Agriculture · crop production and acreage reports · prices
│   └─ Economic Research Service .................. ○ appointed inside USDA
│         Farm income and finance · food security · commodity outlook
│         ⚑ Both are inside the 2026 USDA relocation. NASS staff had until 8 July 2026
│           to accept a move to St. Louis or a regional office and were to report by
│           21 September 2026; a judge granted a temporary pause in September 2026.
│
├─◆ DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION ................................ 1 principal agency
│   └─ National Center for Education Statistics ... ★ ⏳ 6-year term · § 20 U.S.C. 9541
│         NAEP (the Nation's Report Card) · IPEDS · longitudinal studies · school finance
│         ⚑ THE LONGEST STATUTORY TERM OF THE THIRTEEN, AND THE FIRST ONE BROKEN. The
│           commissioner was removed mid-term on 24 February 2025 — the first forcible
│           removal in the office's history. Staff went from roughly 100 to four, then
│           back to about a dozen by mid-2026. Sits inside the Institute of Education
│           Sciences inside a department the administration is winding down.
│
├─◆ DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES ................ 1 principal agency
│   └─ National Center for Health Statistics ...... ○ appointed inside HHS
│         Vital statistics (births and deaths) · NHANES · National Health Interview Survey
│         ⚑ Sits INSIDE the CDC — a statistical agency nested two layers down inside an
│           operating agency, which is a different exposure from sitting beside one.
│
├─◆ DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE .................................. 1 principal agency
│   └─ Bureau of Justice Statistics ............... ◐ President appoints, NO Senate consent
│         National Crime Victimization Survey · prisoner and jail statistics · the federal
│         law enforcement census · court and prosecution statistics
│         ⚑ § 34 U.S.C. 10132(b) REQUIRED SENATE CONFIRMATION UNTIL 2012, when Pub. L.
│           112-166 struck "by and with the advice and consent of the Senate" from the
│           sentence. Congress removed a confirmation requirement from a statistical
│           agency as a housekeeping measure, and nobody has put it back.
│         ⚑ The statute still says the Director "shall be responsible for the integrity
│           of data and statistics" — a duty with no term of office attached to it.
│
├─◆ DEPARTMENT OF ENERGY ................................... 1 principal agency
│   └─ Energy Information Administration .......... ★ no fixed term · § 42 U.S.C. 7135
│         Short-Term Energy Outlook · Annual Energy Outlook · petroleum and power data
│         ⚑ Senate-confirmed but TERMLESS — the mirror image of the other three ★ seats.
│           Confirmation without a term buys scrutiny going in and nothing after.
│
├─◆ DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION ........................... 1 principal agency
│   └─ Bureau of Transportation Statistics ........ ○ competitive service · § 49 U.S.C. 6302
│         Freight and commodity flow · airline data · transportation statistics annual report
│         ⚑ THE STATUTE APPOINTS THIS HEAD INTO THE COMPETITIVE SERVICE. Of the thirteen,
│           this is the only one Congress made a career civil servant on purpose — which
│           is either the strongest protection in the tree or the weakest, depending
│           entirely on what happens to the competitive service.
│
├─◆ DEPARTMENT OF THE TREASURY ............................. 1 principal agency
│   └─ Statistics of Income (IRS) ................. ○ appointed inside Treasury
│         Individual and corporate tax return statistics · migration flows · nonprofit data
│         ⚑ The only one of the thirteen whose raw input is protected by a criminal
│           confidentiality statute of its own (26 U.S.C. 6103) rather than by CIPSEA.
│
├─◆ NATIONAL SCIENCE FOUNDATION ............................ 1 principal agency
│   └─ Nat'l Center for Science and Engineering Stats  ○ appointed inside NSF
│         R&D expenditure · science and engineering indicators · doctorate surveys
│         Runs the Standard Application Process office for the whole system.
│
└─◆ SOCIAL SECURITY ADMINISTRATION ......................... 1 principal agency
    └─ Office of Research, Evaluation, and Statistics  ○ appointed inside SSA
          Beneficiary and earnings statistics · the annual statistical supplement

WHAT ACTUALLY BINDS ALL SIXTEEN — and who can change it
│
├─ STATUTE (Congress changes it)
│    § Paperwork Reduction Act of 1995 — 44 U.S.C. ch. 35 · OMB clearance of collections
│    § Evidence Act of 2018, Title III = CIPSEA 2018 — confidentiality, the four
│      fundamental responsibilities, the Statistical Official in every major agency
│    § 5 C.F.R. Part 1321 — the responsibilities, in regulation
│
└─ DIRECTIVE (OMB changes it, without notice-and-comment in most cases)
     SPD No. 1  · Fundamental Responsibilities — objectivity, independence, trust
     SPD No. 2  · Standards and Guidelines for Statistical Surveys (+ addendum)
     SPD No. 3  · Compilation and Release of Principal Federal Economic Indicators
                  ⚑ This is the one that sets the pre-release lockup rules and bars
                    political commentary on a number for one hour after it is issued.
     SPD No. 4  · Release and Dissemination of Statistical Products
     SPD No. 7  · Metropolitan Statistical Areas
     SPD No. 8  · North American Industry Classification System (NAICS)
     SPD No. 10 · Standard Occupational Classification (SOC)
     SPD No. 15 · Standards for Federal Data on Race and Ethnicity
     ⚑ EIGHT DIRECTIVES IN FORCE, NUMBERED TO 15. The gaps are rescinded or superseded
       instruments — the numbering itself is a record of how easily these are withdrawn.
