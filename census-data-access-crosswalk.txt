■ 0. THE FRAME — THE PENALTY ASYMMETRY THAT SETS EVERY RUNG BELOW
│
├─  ⚖ THE RESPONDENT WHO REFUSES ......... 13 U.S.C. § 221
│      fined not more than $100  ·  false answer: not more than $500
│      [no prison term · effectively never charged · no census prosecution since 1970]
│
├─  ⚖ THE CUSTODIAN WHO DISCLOSES ....... 13 U.S.C. § 214
│      imprisoned not more than 5 YEARS  ·  statute says $5,000
│      [18 U.S.C. § 3571 raises the practical maximum to $250,000 — the figure the
│       Bureau's own confidentiality training and its sworn-status briefings use]
│      ✍ the § 23(c) oath is FOR LIFE. It does not end with employment.
│
└─  ✱ THE RATIO IS THE DESIGN. The system threatens its own staff roughly 2,500x
      harder than it threatens a household that stonewalls it. Every rung below is
      priced and gated to keep the custodian out of prison, not to keep the citizen out.
│
■ RUNG 1 — AGGREGATED TABLES ...................... data.census.gov + the Census API
│      who may ask ....... anyone, no identification, no stated purpose
│      what you get ..... published tables: nation → region → state → county → tract
│                         → block group → block, for the deadlined products
│      $ ................ FREE. API key free on request; no fee tier exists.
│      ⏱ ............... instant
│      ✍ ............... nothing
│      ⚖ ............... none — the data is already disclosure-protected on release
│      ✱ the protection is baked in BEFORE you see it: suppression and coarsening
│        under DAO 216-26, noise infusion in the 2020 products it replaced
│
■ RUNG 2 — INDIVIDUAL RECORDS OF STRANGERS ................................... PUMS
│      who may ask ....... anyone, no identification, no stated purpose
│      what you get ..... the Public Use Microdata Sample: one row per person and
│                         per housing unit — real individual records, deliberately
│                         coarsened rather than aggregated
│      the coarsening ... geography is truncated to the PUMA, which must contain
│                         ≥ 100,000 people at delineation; top-coding on income and
│                         age; rare categories collapsed; PUMAs redrawn each decade
│                         by the STATE DATA CENTERS, not by the Bureau
│      $ ................ FREE
│      ⏱ ............... instant
│      ✍ ............... nothing
│      ✱ THE LADDER'S FIRST INVERSION. This rung gives a stranger individual-level
│        records at no cost and no oath. Rung 3 — your own record — costs $155.
│
■ RUNG 3 — YOUR OWN RECORD, BEFORE IT IS PUBLIC ............. Age Search Service
│      who may ask ....... the NAMED PERSON only, or an heir or legal representative
│      what you get ..... a transcript of your own entry in a census still closed
│                         under the 72-year rule — name, age, place of birth
│      $ ................ $155 per search  [raised from $65, effective Aug 7, 2026]
│                         + $50 expedited surcharge  [raised from $20, same notice]
│      the reason for $ .. 13 U.S.C. § 8(a) requires the service to be SELF-SUPPORTING.
│                         The fee is not a charge for privacy; it is the whole budget.
│                         The statute does NOT authorize a waiver — unlike FOIA.
│      ⏱ ............... weeks, or one day at the surcharge
│      ✍ ............... proof of identity and of relationship where claimed
│      ⚑ [unconfirmed] census.gov search results carry the page title "Age Search
│        Service on Pause" across many unrelated URLs, consistent with a site-wide
│        alert banner. No pause text appears in the fetched page bodies and no
│        Federal Register notice announces one. NOT asserted here. See Notes.
│
■ RUNG 4 — A TABLE THAT DOES NOT EXIST YET .............. Custom Tabulation (ACS)
│      who may ask ....... any organization or individual
│      what you get ..... a cross-tabulation built to your specification from
│                         microdata you will never touch
│      $ ................ $4,000 MINIMUM; priced per request on complexity
│      ⏱ ............... 8 WEEKS minimum — of which 4–6 weeks is DRB review alone
│      ✍ ............... a specification, negotiated and then frozen
│      ◆ the gate ....... the DISCLOSURE REVIEW BOARD. It can refuse, or return the
│        table with cells suppressed, and the requester pays either way. The DRB is
│        an internal Bureau body: no charter in statute, no published docket, no
│        appeal to anyone outside the Bureau.
│      ✱ this is the first rung where a HUMAN BEING decides what you may know.
│
■ RUNG 5 — RESTRICTED MICRODATA ........... SAP → Special Sworn Status → an FSRDC
│      who may ask ....... a researcher with a STATISTICAL PURPOSE. Commercial,
│                         regulatory and ENFORCEMENT purposes are barred outright.
│      what you get ..... the confidential files themselves, inside a secure
│                         facility, on an approved project, and nothing leaves the
│                         room until the DRB clears the output
│
│   ◆ 5a. THE FRONT DOOR ......... Standard Application Process — sap.nsf.gov
│         one form for 16 recognized statistical agencies; hosted by NSF, coordinated
│         by the Interagency Council on Statistical Policy
│         [live] replaced ResearchDataGov.org, which CLOSED PERMANENTLY June 16, 2026
│         ✱ the portal does NOT decide. Each agency decides on its own criteria.
│         ⏱ Census review: 12 WEEKS MAXIMUM from receipt to decision —
│           ≥ 2 subject-matter reviews · Policy Coordination Office · disclosure
│           avoidance review · data availability review
│
│   ◆ 5b. THE OATH ............... SPECIAL SWORN STATUS
│         ✍ background investigation · fingerprinting · the § 23(c) lifetime oath
│         ⏱ 3–4 MONTHS, and it runs AFTER the project is approved, not in parallel
│         ⚑ U.S. residency 36 of the past 60 months, and continued residency
│           throughout — a nationality-adjacent gate on who may study the data
│         ⚖ once sworn, the researcher is inside § 214. Five years, same as staff.
│
│   ◆ 5c. THE ROOM ............... 38 open FSRDC locations  [as of Sept 4, 2026]
│         $ Census Bureau data: NO direct charge to the researcher
│         $ but the FACILITY charges. Consortium-affiliated: often free.
│           NOT affiliated: $15,000 – $20,000 PER PROJECT PER YEAR.
│         $ peer agencies stack on top — NCHS: $3,000 minimum project fee
│           + $1,800 SSS application fee
│         ✱ THE LADDER'S SECOND INVERSION. The statute charges nothing. The
│           UNIVERSITY charges everything. Access is means-tested by institution,
│           not by law — and no statute says so.
│
■ RUNG 6 — THE RECORD BECOMES FULLY PUBLIC .................... THE 72-YEAR RULE
│      who may ask ....... anyone. No fee, no oath, no purpose, no Bureau at all.
│      what you get ..... the actual enumeration sheet, names and all, at the
│                         National Archives
│      $ ................ FREE
│      ⏱ ............... 72 YEARS
│      1950 census ...... released April 1, 2022
│      1960 census ...... due April 1, 2032
│      ◆ where 72 came from: NOT a privacy judgment. In 1942 the 1870 schedules
│        were transferred to the Archives — 72 years after the count, by accident of
│        filing. The 1952 Peel–Grover agreement (Oct 10, 1952) fixed that interval
│        as policy; Pub. L. 95-416 (Oct 5, 1978) ratified the 1952 agreement in
│        statute. Congress codified an accident and no one has revisited the number.
│
■ THE WALL — WHAT NO RUNG ON THIS LADDER REACHES
│
├─  ✕ FOIA ............... §§ 8(b) and 9(a) are valid Exemption 3 withholding
│      statutes. Raw census data cannot be FOIA'd, and no public-interest balancing
│      applies — Baldrige v. Shapiro, 455 U.S. 345 (1982), UNANIMOUS.
│
├─  ✕ CIVIL DISCOVERY .... the same provisions create a statutory PRIVILEGE under
│      Rule 26(b)(1). Relevance does not defeat it. Necessity does not defeat it.
│
├─  ✕ THE MASTER ADDRESS LIST — the sharpest edge of the holding. Baldrige held
│      address lists ARE raw census data, and municipalities could not obtain their
│      OWN lists even pledging statistical use only. The single most useful thing a
│      city could have for fixing its own undercount is the thing most firmly locked.
│
├─  ✕ ANY OTHER AGENCY .... § 9 bars use for any non-statistical purpose. Law
│      enforcement, immigration, taxation and regulation all sit outside the door.
│
└─  ✕ USE AGAINST THE RESPONDENT — the Bureau's own formulation: the information
      "cannot be used against you by any government agency or court."
      ✱ This wall is the STRONGEST confidentiality guarantee in the federal
        government — stronger than tax return data, stronger than medical records
        held by federal agencies. It is also the reason Rungs 3, 4 and 5 cost what
        they cost. The price of the ladder IS the price of the wall.
