STATISTICAL OFFICIALS CROSSWALK — 24 AGENCIES, ONE TITLE, TWO ENTIRELY DIFFERENT JOBS
│   Legend: ★ Senate-confirmed   ⏳ fixed statutory term   ○ appointed inside the department
│           § statute   ⊘ stated in law, no authority attached   ◎ statute stops, discretion starts
│           ≠ OMB's own documents disagree   ◈ this page's arithmetic   ⚑ structural point
│   Time-slice: roster read 23 Oct 2026 · statute as in force Oct 2026

■ PART A — WHAT THE STATUTE ACTUALLY SAYS
│
├─◆ § ................ 5 U.S.C. § 314, "Statistical expertise"
│   │                  Added by Evidence Act § 101(a)(2), P.L. 115-435 (2019).
│   │   who owes one .. "each agency" = the 24 agencies listed at 31 U.S.C. § 901(b),
│   │                   by way of the definition at 5 U.S.C. § 311(1).
│   │   ⚑ THE DUTY ATTACHES TO A HARD-CODED LIST OF AGENCIES, NOT TO AGENCIES
│   │     THAT STILL EXIST. See USAID in Part C.
│   │
│   ├─  THE ONE MANDATORY VERB IN THE SECTION:
│   │     "The head of each agency SHALL DESIGNATE ..."
│   │     — and designation is the entire obligation. Nothing follows from it.
│   │
│   ├─  THE FORK, VERBATIM, WHICH IS WHY PARTS B AND C ARE DIFFERENT JOBS:   ◎
│   │     "... the head of any statistical agency or unit within the agency,
│   │      or in the case of an agency that does not have a statistical agency
│   │      or unit, any senior agency official with appropriate expertise ..."
│   │
│   └─  WHAT THE OFFICIAL MAY THEN DO:
│         "... as a statistical official to advise on statistical policy,
│          techniques, and procedures. Agency officials engaged in statistical
│          activities MAY CONSULT with any such statistical official
│          AS NECESSARY."
│       ⊘ ADVISE IS THE WHOLE POWER. The consulting duty is permissive, it is
│         owed BY other officials rather than TO anyone, and "as necessary" is
│         judged by the official deciding whether to consult.
│       ⊘ NO TERM. NO FOR-CAUSE REMOVAL CLAUSE. NO REPORTING LINE. NO BUDGET
│         LINE. NO APPEAL IF OVERRULED. NO CONSEQUENCE IF NEVER CONSULTED.
│
├─◆ WHERE THE JOB'S ACTUAL CONTENT LIVES — AND IT IS NOT IN THE STATUTE        ⚑
│   │   OMB Memorandum M-19-23, 10 July 2019, App. C. It supplies everything
│   │   § 314 omits:
│   │   ├─ "Agency champion for data quality"
│   │   ├─ "Agency champion for confidentiality protection and appropriate
│   │   │   data access"
│   │   ├─ a seat on the agency's Data Governance Body
│   │   ├─ "the standing and authority to perform the functions" and
│   │   │   "sufficient independence"
│   │   └─ fn. 31: "it is OMB's expectation that THESE ARE FULL-TIME POSITIONS"
│   │   ⚑ A MEMORANDUM CAN BE RESCINDED WITHOUT LEGISLATION. Strip M-19-23 and
│   │     § 314 still stands, having lost nothing, because the memo is carrying
│   │     the entire job description. Compare Statistical Policy Directive No. 1,
│   │     which does the same load-bearing work for the agencies themselves.
│   │   Designation deadline was 13 July 2019; names public by 2 Aug 2019.
│
└─◆ THE HEADLINE NUMBER IS ARITHMETICALLY TRUE AND SUBSTANTIVELY WRONG         ◈
      OMB and the ICSP both say 24 Statistical Officials. The roster carries 24
      labels. But:
      ├─ USAID's seat reads "Vacant" ................. 1 agency with NONE
      ├─ HUD carries TWO named officials ............. 1 agency with TWO
      └─ 23 agencies filled + 1 empty = 24 agencies; 24 labels across
         23 filled agencies. The duplicate cancels the vacancy and the
         published total comes out right.                                      ◈

└─◆ THE SHAPE                                                                  ⚑
      In Part B the title is the smallest thing the person holds. In Part C it
      is frequently the only thing. Both are "the Statistical Official" in the
      same sentence of the same statute, counted in the same total of 24, and
      seated on the same Interagency Council on Statistical Policy — whose
      chair, the Chief Statistician of the United States, is himself not
      Senate-confirmed. So the protection a federal statistic enjoys at a given
      agency is not set by § 314 at all. It is set by whether that agency
      happened to already have a bureau when the Evidence Act passed.
