FEDERAL CIVILIAN WORKFORCE — SERVICE STRUCTURE & REMOVAL PROTECTIONS (as of Aug 16, 2026)
│   ~2.0–2.1M civilian employees excluding the Postal Service — lowest since the mid-1960s
│   Executive-branch employment fell ~281,000 (≈12%) between Jan 2025 and Mar 2026 (OPM)
│   ★ = full MSPB appeal rights   ✗ = no MSPB appeal   ◐ = contested / newly narrowed
│
■ THE FEDERAL CIVILIAN WORKFORCE
│
├─◆ COMPETITIVE SERVICE ★            the merit system — Pendleton 1883, rebuilt by CSRA 1978
│   │   Entry by competitive examination · veterans' preference · "for cause" removal only
│   │
│   ├─ General Schedule .............. GS-1 through GS-15, the pay & classification spine
│   ├─ Federal Wage System ........... trade, craft, and labor positions
│   └─ Probationers ◐ ................ no appeal rights until tenure vests
│         [EO 14284, Apr 24 2025 — tenure no longer vests by default; an affirmative
│          retention decision is now required 30 days out or the appointment ends]
│
├─◆ EXCEPTED SERVICE                  positions excepted from competitive examination
│   │   by statute, by OPM regulation, or by presidential order — seven schedules
│   │
│   ├─ Schedule A ★ ......... examination impracticable (agency attorneys · chaplains ·
│   │                          the 213.3102(u) disability-hiring authority)
│   ├─ Schedule B ★ ......... noncompetitive examination; specialized or critical need
│   ├─ Schedule C ✗ ......... POLITICAL — confidential or policy-determining, GS-15 and
│   │                          below; serve at the pleasure of the appointing official
│   ├─ Schedule D ★ ......... Pathways — interns, recent graduates, Presidential
│   │                          Management Fellows
│   ├─ Schedule E ◐ ......... Administrative Law Judges [excepted by EO 13843, Jul 2018;
│   │                          removal still requires an MSPB good-cause finding,
│   │                          5 U.S.C. § 7521 — the protection survived the reclass.]
│   ├─ Schedule POLICY/CAREER ✗ ...... FORMERLY SCHEDULE F
│   │      Career employees, merit-hired, in "policy-influencing" positions — who keep
│   │      competitive status but lose adverse-action procedure and MSPB appeal.
│   │      EO 13957 (Oct 21 2020) → rescinded by EO 14003 (Jan 2021) →
│   │      EO 14171 (Jan 20 2025, renamed, designation moved to the President) →
│   │      OPM final rule published Feb 5 2026, effective Mar 9 2026 →
│   │      EO 14410 (Jun 3 2026) — the transfer actually happens
│   │      [≈8,000 positions in a 229-page appendix; ~97% at or above GS-15;
│   │       the GS-13/14 exceptions are concentrated at OMB. OPM's rulemaking
│   │       estimate was ~50,000; earlier outside estimates ran to 200,000.]
│   └─ Schedule G ✗ ......... NONCAREER policy-making / policy-advocating positions that
│                              turn over with a presidential transition
│                              [created by EO 14317, Jul 17 2025 — a second political
│                               layer above Schedule C; appointment and withdrawal run
│                               through the White House Office of Presidential Personnel]
│
├─◆ SENIOR EXECUTIVE SERVICE ◐       the corps between career staff and appointees
│   │   6,647 members (Apr 2026) · statutory cap: noncareer ≤10% government-wide,
│   │   ≤25% within any one agency · removal and demotion run through a separate track
│   │
│   ├─ Career SES ............ competitively selected, certified by a Qualifications
│   │                           Review Board; MSPB rights are narrower than GS
│   ├─ Noncareer SES ✗ ....... politically appointed, no QRB, serve at pleasure
│   ├─ Limited term · limited emergency ..... time-capped appointments
│   └─ [The lever here is REASSIGNMENT, not removal: 15-day notice, a 120-day
│        moratorium after a new agency head arrives — and then a career executive
│        can be sent anywhere in the country. OPM memos of Jan 20 2025 and
│        Dec 1 2025 both urged agencies to use it.]
│
├─◆ PRESIDENTIAL APPOINTEES ✗        outside the civil service entirely
│   ├─ PAS ... Presidential Appointment, Senate confirmation required (~1,200 seats)
│   ├─ PA .... Presidential Appointment, no Senate vote (~800 seats)
│   └─ [PAS + PA + noncareer SES + Schedule C + Schedule G = the "Plum Book" layer,
│        historically ~4,000 positions and now growing by the addition of Schedule G]
│
├─◆ SEPARATE PERSONNEL SYSTEMS       statutorily outside the Title 5 competitive service
│   │   Their own hiring, pay, and appeal regimes — reached by different levers
│   ├─ Foreign Service (State) · VA Title 38 clinicians · FBI · the intelligence agencies
│   ├─ TSA screeners · FAA · Postal Service · TVA · Federal Reserve Board staff
│   └─ Legislative & judicial branch staff · GAO · the Article III judiciary
│

    THE EXIT RAMPS — five ways a career position stops being a protected career position
    ├─ 1. Reduction in force ..... abolish the position; competitive-area rules decide who
    ├─ 2. Deferred resignation ... the "Fork in the Road" offer, Jan–Feb 2025
    ├─ 3. Probationary firing .... no appeal; the default flipped by EO 14284
    ├─ 4. Reclassification ....... competitive service ──▶ Schedule Policy/Career (EO 14410)
    └─ 5. Function transfer ...... move the work to another agency and leave the box empty
